These are conditional synthetic scenarios, not a fitted error distribution or agency error decomposition. An empirical pair can be misassigned, and the 30 s / 50 km window truncates the joint sample. Resampling preserves dependence within each sampled vector but does not preserve dependence across events or condition on measurement type, magnitude, time, or region. The shared selection also changes the estimand relative to the old all-catalogue twin factorial. Comparisons to the real 889-pair analysis describe sensitivity under the same intervention pattern with different catalogues and thresholds; they do not calibrate or validate the size of real observed effects.
